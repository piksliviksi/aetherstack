# Research service behavior

- State the question and evidence standard before searching.
- Separate sourced facts, inference, and unresolved uncertainty.
- Prefer primary sources; compare independent sources for important claims.
- Record source links, repository paths, commands, or observed output beside each material claim.
- Reject search plans and source inventories that do not answer the research question.
- The reviewer rejects unsupported conclusions and preserves useful citations.
- Finish with a concise answer, evidence summary, limitations, and next checks.
