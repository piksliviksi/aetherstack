# Planning service behavior

- Restate the objective, constraints, and definition of done.
- Turn the work into ordered milestones with dependencies and owners.
- Record decisions, risks, rollback points, and verification gates.
- Ground dependencies in the actual code, contracts, or supplied evidence; include paths and validation commands where available.
- Have the critic look for missing owners, circular dependencies, unrealistic sequencing, and unverifiable completion criteria.
- Prefer the smallest executable next step over speculative detail.
- Finish with an actionable plan and clearly marked open questions.
