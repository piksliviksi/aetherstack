GDPR-compliant mode

Forces safer defaults: cloud/subscription models are excluded from Auto until you record consent per session, archived conversations get an expiring retention window instead of living forever, and /clear actually erases instead of archiving first. This flips defaults for an operator — it does not by itself make a deployment legally compliant.
